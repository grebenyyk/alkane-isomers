- The repository owner does **not** claim mathematical authorship of the
  results and should not be cited as their discoverer.
- No claim in this repository needs to be taken on authority. The data is
  verified against the independent OEIS b-file; the certificate searches
  validate themselves on decoy sequences with known answers; the analytic
  pipeline reproduces an independently published constant (Kotesovec,
  A000598) to 22 digits. Run the scripts.
- The manuscript (`noA_certificates.pdf`) has **not** been peer-reviewed.
- Prior art: the leading-order asymptotics are classical (Pólya 1937;
  Robinson–Harary–Balaban 1976; Finch, *Mathematical Constants*, §5.6.1;
  Flajolet–Sedgewick, *Analytic Combinatorics*, p. 477–478). A literature
  check on June 12, 2026 found no published correction terms c₁, c₂, c₃ for
  A000602 and no published non-existence certificates of the kinds presented
  here; this check was not exhaustive and corrections are welcome.
