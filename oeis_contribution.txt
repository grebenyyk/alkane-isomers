PART B — suggested "comment to editors" for the proposal form
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

----------------------------------------------------------------------
The leading form C*d^n/n^(5/2) is classical (Polya 1937; constants discussed in
Robinson-Harary-Balaban 1976 and Finch, Mathematical Constants, Section 5.6.1,
both already cited in this entry). The numerical values were recomputed as
follows: exact terms a(0)..a(620) were generated from the standard functional
equations (they agree with the full b-file by P. von Broemssen, terms 0..1000);
d = 1/rho where rho solves the smooth-implicit-function system for the g.f. of
A000598 (Newton iteration at 60-digit working precision, residual < 10^-55);
c, c1, c2 were obtained by Richardson/Neville extrapolation of
a(n)*rho^n*n^(5/2) on two disjoint node sets (n = 240..600 step 30, and
n = 200..600 step 40), which agree to 1.7*10^-18 for c, ~10^-13 (relative) for
c1, and ~5*10^-12 (relative) for c2; all quoted digits are stable across both
node sets. As an end-to-end validation, the identical pipeline reproduces
Kotesovec's published constant on A000598 (0.51787590645889353699316235699...)
to 22 significant digits. Computations were AI-assisted (scripts available on
request / at the repository linked in my profile).

PART D — how to submit (you have not contributed to OEIS before)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Register at https://oeis.org/wiki/Special:RequestAccount — real names are
   required by OEIS policy; account approval usually takes a day or two.
2. Once logged in, go to https://oeis.org/A000602, click "edit" (this creates
   a draft proposal, it does not change the live entry).
3. Add the Part A text as a new line in the FORMULA section, placed after the
   existing g.f. lines. Your signature "- _YOUR NAME_, Jun 12 2026" is added
   automatically if you use the four-tilde shortcut, or type it manually in
   exactly that format.
4. Paste the Part B text into the pink "comment to editors" box.
5. Submit. Editors typically respond within days; they may ask for the digits
   to be trimmed/extended or for the wording to be tightened — both are
   normal parts of the review.

Notes on norms: numerically computed asymptotic constants are a standard,
welcomed category of OEIS contribution (V. Kotesovec alone has thousands).
The contributor vouches that the computation is correct and reproducible —
which you can: the scripts in this repository regenerate every digit.
AI assistance does not need to be hidden; mentioning it in the editor comment
(as Part B does) is honest and unproblematic, since the contribution is a
verifiable numerical fact, not a claim of creative authorship.
