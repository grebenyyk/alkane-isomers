PART B — suggested "comment to editors" for the proposal form
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

----------------------------------------------------------------------
The leading form c*d^n/n^(5/2) is classical (Polya 1937; Robinson-Harary-
Balaban 1976; Finch, Mathematical Constants, Section 5.6.1 — all already
cited in this entry). The growth constant d matches Kotesovec's value on
A000598 (d = 1/A261340); note the exponent here is 5/2 (unrooted), versus 3/2
on A000598 (rooted), and the amplitude c is the unrooted one (Finch gives
0.6563186958; extended here). Computation: exact terms a(0)..a(620) were
generated from the standard functional equations (they agree with the full
b-file by P. von Broemssen, terms 0..1000); rho was recomputed by Newton
iteration at 60-digit working precision (residual < 10^-55) and agrees with
A261340 to all 42 computed digits; c, c1, c2 were obtained by
Richardson/Neville extrapolation of a(n)*rho^n*n^(5/2) on two disjoint node
sets (n = 240..600 step 30, and n = 200..600 step 40), agreeing to 1.7*10^-18
for c and ~10^-11 (absolute) for c1, c2; all quoted digits are stable across
both node sets. As an end-to-end validation, the identical pipeline reproduces
Kotesovec's published amplitude on A000598 to 22 significant digits.
Computations were AI-assisted (scripts available on request / at the
repository linked in my profile).
